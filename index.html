FAQ

Real Questions, Real Answers

Everythin' folks ask before dey book.

How far ahead I gotta book?

Two weeks minimum. Summer dates an' holidays go fast — da sooner you tap in, da better yo shot at lockin' yo date.

How much is da deposit?

50% of da total locks yo date. Da balance is due da day of da event. No date is held without a deposit — dat's how we keep it fair fo' everybody.

What's da minimum party size?

25 guests. Smaller than dat, ask anyway — if da date open, we might can work somethin' out.

Can I taste da food first?

Yeah — tastings available fo' weddings an' any event over 100 guests, once yo date is penciled in.

What about dietary needs?

Vegetarian, vegan, an' halal options on request. Tell us when you book an' we'll build dem into yo menu — nobody at yo function get left out.

What's included in every package?

Da food, serving staff, setup, serving equipment, an' full cleanup. Rentals like tables, chairs an' linens can be coordinated — included free in Da Whole Foe Nem package.

Do y'all travel outside Chicago?

Yeah — all suburbs. A travel fee applies past 25 miles an' it's quoted up front in yo estimate, never snuck in later.
