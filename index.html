How It Works

Three steps to event day

No chaos, no surprises — a clear path from first call to final toast.

01

Consult

Tell us the date, the headcount, and the feeling you're after. We draft a menu and a straight, itemized quote.

02

Taste

You taste the menu, we adjust it together, and we lock the timeline, rentals, and staffing plan.

03

Gather

We arrive early, set the room, cook, pour, and serve — then clean up like we were never there.

Plan your event

Tell us about your gathering and we'll come back with menu ideas and a quote. The more you share — date, venue, headcount, favorites — the sharper the first draft.
